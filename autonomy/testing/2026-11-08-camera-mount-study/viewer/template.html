Camera model. The ideal rectified pinhole from lhr_vehicle/config/vehicle.yaml: 1280 × 720, 110° horizontal, square pixels, so fx = fy = 448 px and 77.5° vertical. Stereolabs quotes 110° × 70° for the 2.1 mm lens, so the real image is a little shorter than this. Lenses sit 60 mm either side of the rig centre.

    
Colouring a cone. camera_lidar_fusion projects each LiDAR-mapped cone at 0.1625 m into the left image. It needs that point in frame and unblocked, within 20 m (max_color_range_m), and an HSV patch of at least 25 px (min_region_area). Pixels here are the whole cone silhouette, so they are an upper bound: stripes, shading and motion blur shrink the patch.

    
Car geometry. orion.stl in lhr_vehicle/meshes, from the SOLIDWORKS full-car assembly. No driver, helmet or head restraint, and no camera bracket or cable. Cones do not block each other. The real space claim belongs in the SOLIDWORKS assembly.

    
Rules and reference points. DT.2.3 keeps driverless parts inside the primary-structure volume, so the camera top is checked against the main hoop top at 1.115 m (front face x = −0.995 m). Distances are along the ground past the nose tip (x = 0.955 m). The 1.5 m edge offset is half the 3 m minimum track width.
