Car geometry. orion.stl in lhr_vehicle/meshes, exported from the SOLIDWORKS full-car assembly _26-000.SLDASM (SVN r9480) and reduced on a 20 mm grid. Nothing sits more than 13.7 mm outside the real surface. Parts under 40 mm across were dropped.

Not in the model. No driver, helmet or head restraint, and no LiDAR bracket or cable. A gap narrower than the 20 mm grid may show as blocked or open. The real space claim belongs in the SOLIDWORKS assembly.

Beams. All 800,000 directions in Livox's Mid-360 scan table (the one lhr_lidar_sim replays), −7.2° to +52.2°, 20,000 beams per 10 Hz frame. Points on a cone are density times the cone's size as seen from the sensor, before range dropout. Against lhr_lidar_sim's full model this agrees within about 20%, except where the densest band lands, which the full model shows up to about 40% higher.

Main hoop. Top at 1.115 m, front face at x = −0.995 m, on the centreline. The rear wing reaches 1.083 m right behind it. Distances are along the ground past the nose tip (x = 0.955 m).
